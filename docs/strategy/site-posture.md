# Site Posture Strategy

## Purpose

This document is the source of truth for The Commissioner’s Office website claims, CTA language, public roadmap posture, and current-vs-future visual labeling.

Use this document before changing public website copy, CTA language, public roadmap language, visual labels, screenshot captions, future-comp framing, metadata, or privacy-adjacent public copy.

## Current Approved Positioning Statement

The Commissioner’s Office builds premium sports franchise simulations. Front Office Baseball, the first title, has reached a playable private MVP — a small early tester group is running franchises in a live build on the web. Access is deliberately limited while the core loop is refined. The playable MVP is the proven core; richer interface and product direction shown in design explorations are direction, not shipped product.

## Approved Stage Facts

These facts are approved for public-site posture and copy:

- Front Office Baseball is playable on the internet.
- It is a private MVP / private playtest, not a public launch.
- A small early tester group has already been contacted.
- Access is deliberately limited.
- The playable core loop includes choosing a team, reading the league, advancing time, evaluating players, managing roster/franchise context, and building early attachment.
- The current MVP uses an 8-team fictional quick-start.
- Future feedback rounds may be drawn from the development list.
- No fixed public launch timeline is promised.

## Forbidden or High-Risk Claims

The following claims are forbidden or high-risk for public copy unless separately approved later. These phrases may appear in this document only as examples of what not to claim.

- public launch
- release
- beta
- commercial completeness
- commercially complete
- immediate playtest access
- sign up to play
- “play now”
- “available now”
- 30 teams
- 162 games
- multiplayer
- modding
- public accounts
- downloads
- launch dates
- future visual comps presented as shipped gameplay

## Canonical Vocabulary

Use this vocabulary consistently across the website.

- Immutable tagline: “Run your franchise. Build a dynasty. Shape history.”
- Status chip: “Playable Private MVP”
- Short/mobile status if needed: “Private MVP”
- Current screenshot label: “Current MVP Build”
- Future visual labels: “Future Interface Direction” and “Design Exploration”
- Future comp naming convention: `fob-vision-*.png`
- Standing disclaimer for future comps: “Design exploration — not final gameplay UI; no commitment to specific features, scope, or dates.”

## CTA and Funnel Language

Primary CTA:

- “Join the Development List”

Tally URL:

- `https://tally.so/r/OD8BZM`

Approved supporting microcopy:

> Front Office Baseball is in private MVP playtesting with a small early group. The development list is where updates land first — and where future feedback rounds will be drawn from. Access is limited and not immediate.

Avoid bare “Join the Playtest” button language. The site can say future feedback rounds may be drawn from the development list, but it must not imply immediate access.

## Tone Rules

The website should sound:

- premium
- restrained
- credible indie sports sim
- confident but not fake-AAA

Avoid:

- generic startup/SaaS language
- “founding members”
- “be the first”
- “early access” positioning unless specifically approved later

## Visual Truth Rules

Current MVP screenshots and future comps must not be mixed without clear labels.

Current screenshots can be described as real/current product only if they come from the playable MVP.

Future comps must be labeled as design direction, not shipped gameplay. Future comp sections need the standing disclaimer:

> Design exploration — not final gameplay UI; no commitment to specific features, scope, or dates.

Future comp filenames should follow:

- `fob-vision-*.png`

Do not blur shipped MVP evidence and future vision. Do not paraphrase or summarize internal screenshot-archive or roadmap documents into public-facing claims; posture rules only.

## Review Checklist

Before changing public site copy, CTA language, public roadmap copy, metadata, privacy-adjacent copy, screenshot captions, or visual labels, confirm:

- Is the claim supported by the approved stage facts?
- Does the copy preserve “private MVP” rather than implying public launch?
- Does the copy avoid forbidden/high-risk claims outside a clearly labeled internal rule section?
- Does the CTA remain “Join the Development List”?
- Does supporting CTA copy make clear that access is limited and not immediate?
- Are current MVP screenshots labeled as current build evidence?
- Are future comps labeled as direction or design exploration?
- Does every future-comp section include the standing disclaimer?
- Does the copy avoid unapproved public timelines, public accounts, downloads, 30-team claims, 162-game claims, multiplayer, modding, or other future-scope promises?
- Does the change avoid leaking internal roadmap or screenshot-archive detail?
